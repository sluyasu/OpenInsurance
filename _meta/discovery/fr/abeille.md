## Carrier

Abeille Assurances is a brand over first-party carriers of the AEMA group (with Macif and Aésio); it is not an intermediary fronting a third party, so is_carrier is true. Each document names its own carrier and three distinct attributions appear across the library. (1) IARD, santé, scolaire and the professional/agricultural lines, 36 documents: 'Compagnie : Abeille IARD & Santé, Société anonyme au capital de 344 822 425,00 € - Entreprise régie par le Code des assurances, Siège social : 13 rue du Moulin Bailly - 92270 Bois-Colombes - 306 522 665 R.C.S. Nanterre'. (2) Prévoyance, obsèques, dépendance and most savings contracts, 31 documents: 'Abeille Vie, Société anonyme d'Assurances Vie et de Capitalisation, SA au capital social de 1 205 528 532,67 euros, 732 020 805 RCS Nanterre', 70 avenue de l'Europe, Bois-Colombes. (3) The two AFER contracts are co-insured — the DIC names Abeille Vie and Abeille Épargne Retraite and adds 'ci-après dénommés les « coassureurs »'. The fourth ACPR-registered entity, Abeille Retraite Professionnelle, carries no document in this set: it appears only on the PER/PERO marketing summaries that are excluded as non-contractual. Two attribution traps: the printed share capital of Abeille IARD & Santé varies between editions (317 752 761,16 € vs 344 822 425,00 €) and is an edition marker, not a different entity; and the obsèques DICs mention Abeille IARD & Santé only as the provider of an ancillary 'Convention d'assistance' — the risk carrier there is Abeille Vie, per 'dont Abeille Vie est l'assureur'.

## What this insurer does not publish

Abeille Assurances does not publish conditions générales, and publishes almost no notice d'information, for its retail contracts. The single full contractual text in the whole library is the assurance scolaire notice (17076), printed 'Notice d'information — Année scolaire 2026/2027 (Extrait des Conditions Générales N° 4118)' — and even that is an extract, not the conditions générales themselves. For every other branch — auto, habitation, santé, prévoyance, GAV, obsèques, dépendance, cyber, and the professional, construction and agricultural lines — the public library stops at the two-page IPID/DIPA, which itself says the full terms are 'dans la documentation précontractuelle et contractuelle' without linking them. For assurance vie and épargne there are likewise no conditions générales and no contract-level notice d'information: the master document is the PRIIPs DIC, supplemented by an annexe financière listing eligible supports. Conditions particulières and conditions tarifaires are not published at all, which is expected since they are per-customer. Three things I could not establish, stated plainly rather than guessed. (1) Retraite: not one PER/PERO contract DIC is published. The contracts demonstrably exist — Abeille Retraite Plurielle, Premium Retraite Active, Abeille Plurielle Retraite Entreprise all have a live annexe financière — but the only files the site labels as their 'Document d'informations clés' (D13_ARP, D12_ARPE) contain no PRIIPs section at all and one prints 'Document non contractuel à caractère publicitaire'. So the retraite range has zero contractual documents here, and I cannot tell whether the real DICs are unlinked or simply not published online. (2) No IPID or DIC was found for assurance emprunteur, nor for protection juridique sold on its own, though the site's editorial pages mention both; whether Abeille publishes none or merely does not link them is unknown — no URL space was probed to find out, per the discovery rules. (3) The obsèques DIC page links three documents that 404 (V6732, V6734, v6731) whose anchors name three products — CONVENTION OBSÈQUES, CONVENTION OBSÈQUES PRIMES TEMPORAIRES and CAPITAL PRÉVOYANCE FUNÉRAIRE — so those three obsèques contracts are advertised on the site with no reachable document at all, and what Abeille currently publishes for them is unknown.

## Traps (do not re-derive)

1. curl gets 403, Python urllib/httpx with a browser UA gets 200 on the same URL. fetch_tier is plain-urllib-only.
2. Documents are not linked as .pdf files. They are served from /abdoc/<CODE> with no extension, so a crawler filtering hrefs on '.pdf' finds almost nothing on this site. Match on '/abdoc/' instead.
3. There is no aggregated document library. Apart from the PRIIPs page, each IPID is linked from its own product page only, which is why discovery had to crawl all 936 sitemap URLs to reach 69 contractual documents.
4. Edition dates: the PRIIPs DICs print 'Date de publication'; the IPIDs print their editorial reference in the body ('18764-0426', 'V6359G-0124') whose last four digits are MMYY. Both beat the Content-Disposition filename, which can be stale — document 18821 prints 18821-0124 while its filename says 18821-0123. Records carry an edition_note wherever the two disagree. Two DICs (V7281, v7282) print two dates themselves: 'Date de publication 01/06/2022' with a cover stamp of (01/2023).
5. Rebrand: Aviva France became Abeille Assurances in 2021-2022. Traces survive inside the current library — the prévoyance IPID V6039 is served as 'V6039D 0124 DD-5737 -DIPA Aviva solution prevoyance pro-1.pdf' while the document itself prints the Abeille name, and an Abeille Vie 2022 bond information memorandum narrates the change. Same insurer, one slug; the name is recorded as each document prints it. No legacy aviva.fr asset path is linked from the live site — /abdoc/ is the single current document store — so unlike the sibling insurer whose legacy tree died at a CMS migration, no dead legacy document tree exists here to chase.
6. Assurance vie, épargne and retraite publish no conditions générales. The contract-level master document is the PRIIPs 'Document d'informations clés' (DIC/KID), one per contract, mapped to document_type=ipid because it is the standardised pre-contractual key-information document, the PRIIPs analogue of the IPID. The field printed_kind on every record says which of the two the document actually is: 44 IPIDs, 24 DICs, 1 notice.
7. Biggest trap on this insurer: per-fund and per-option PRIIPs sheets are byte-for-byte the same genre as a contract DIC. 35 were excluded in two families. (a) 18 per-fund sheets keyed by ISIN (FR0013522208…) or by an internal FIC######### code, filenames often 'DIS-…', linked from the cotations and PRIIPs pages — one per unit-linked support. (b) 17 per-option sheets whose printed product is 'GESTION SOUS MANDAT – ORIENTATION DE GESTION PRUDENTE / ÉQUILIBRÉE / DYNAMIQUE', codes ET*, being 3 risk profiles per asset manager (OFI Invest, Rothschild, Flornoy, Amundi…). Ingesting either family would show a handful of contracts as dozens of products. Both are listed in excluded_documents with an exclude_reason.
8. Site label vs document, and the document wins — twice, materially. The PRIIPs and PERO pages link two files anchored 'Document d'informations clés du contrat Abeille Retraite Plurielle' and 'Document d'informations clés du PERO interentreprises' (codes D13_ARP, D12_ARPE). Neither contains a single mandatory PRIIPs section — no risk indicator, no 'Quels sont les risques', no 'Combien de temps dois-je le conserver' — and D12_ARPE prints 'Document non contractuel à caractère publicitaire à jour au 10/10/2022'. They are marketing summaries, not DICs, and are excluded. 24 of the 69 kept documents also have site_label_diverges=true, usually because the anchor is a generic 'Document d'information sur le produit' while the document names the product.
9. 'Annexe – liste des supports éligibles au contrat' (codes *_ANNEXE_FINANCIERE and *_ANNEXE_EXTRA_FINANCIERE, 34 files) is named like a contract annex but contains only a fund list with per-line fees; excluded as non-contractual.
10. Several IPIDs and DICs do not carry their header on the first extracted page — PyMuPDF returns body text first. Detecting the document kind from only the first ~2500 characters silently loses 7 real contractual documents (D12_ARPE aside: V5982, V5984, V5985, V6039, v8565, v8566 and 18749). Detect over the full text.
11. PyMuPDF returns multi-column PDFs in inconsistent reading order, so a plain difflib ratio between two editions of the same document can read as low as 0.67 and look like two different products. Compare sorted token bags instead: the same pair then scores 0.97. This is what separates the two PREMIUM EPARGNE ACTIVE files (0.97, two editions) from the two Suli Prévoyance Obsèques files (0.59, genuine parallel variants — Option Épargne vs Option Prévoyance, distinguished inside the documents only by their internal refs V7281A / V7282A).
12. One live page leaks a pre-production host: /conseils-en-assurance/mes-proches/dic-assurances-obseques-deces.html links https://pprd.abeille-assurances.fr/abdoc/V7512 instead of www. The preprod host answers 200 and serves the identical bytes. It is the only non-www document URL in 345, and the www copy is kept as canonical — do not ingest the pprd hostname.
13. Four /abdoc/ codes linked from live pages are dead (404): UV4801 on the annexe-financiere page, and V6732, V6734, v6731 on the obsèques DIC page. They are in fetch_failures.
14. The /abdoc/ endpoint links some codes in both casings: V8606/v8606, V8607/v8607 and V8608/v8608 each return the same bytes under two URLs, and the dead link 'v6731' is lowercase where its siblings are uppercase. Any downstream store keyed on the code on a case-insensitive filesystem will collide these pairs.
15. By count the site is dominated by non-contractual PDFs — 267 of 336 unique files: agricultural fire and heat-stress leaflets, storm advice, benefit tables, fund performance reportings, SFDR/PAI reports. 51 print an explicit 'Document non contractuel' line, which is the single most reliable discriminator.
