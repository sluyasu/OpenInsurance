## Carrier

Groupama is not an intermediary brand — the risk sits inside the group — but the published documents deliberately do NOT name which regional mutual carries it. The non-life contract documents all print the same substitution formula: « La Caisse Locale d’Assurances Mutuelles Agricoles dénommée ci-après GROUPAMA, (identifiée aux Conditions Personnelles) ayant souscrit un traité de Réassurance emportant substitution auprès de : La Caisse Régionale d’Assurances Mutuelles Agricoles (identifiée aux Conditions Personnelles) » (Profil élus DDG p.1 and Tableau des montants p.1; the Flotte Automobile CG says the same with « Conditions Particulières »). The IPID goes further and prints only « Compagnie : GROUPAMA – Entreprise d’assurance française ». The named entity is therefore resolved per policy in the Conditions Personnelles/Particulières, which are never published. Conclusion for the slug question: ONE slug, 'groupama', for the non-life documents — splitting into 11 regional slugs would be inventing information the documents do not carry. Life, retirement, obsèques and the TNS prévoyance examples are different: they name 'Groupama Gan Vie – Société Anonyme au capital de 1 371 100 605 € – 340 427 616 RCS Paris' as assureur, so those documents belong to the Groupama Gan Vie carrier, not to a régionale. The health CCSF tables name 'Groupama Assurances Mutuelles, pour le compte des Caisses Régionales d’Assurances Mutuelles Agricoles' (the group holding acting for the régionales). Assistance in the Profil élus and Flotte contracts is carried by Mutuaide Assistance. The only documents on the site that DO name an individual régionale are the certificats mutualistes notices (Groupama Grand Est, Groupama Rhône-Alpes Auvergne) — but those are securities notices, not insurance contracts, and are excluded from the list below.

## What this insurer does not publish

No conditions générales, no notices d'information and no IPIDs for any retail line: auto, moto, vélo/EDPM, habitation, santé individuelle, GAV, scolaire, chasse, navigation, voyage, animaux, dépendance, emprunteur, protection juridique. No conditions particulières of any kind. Nothing contractual for the agricole line at all (no multirisque agricole, no récolte/grêle, no engins/tracteurs), even though Groupama is France's main agricultural insurer. Nothing contractual for the professionnels/entreprise lines currently on the site either — the only CG that exist (flotte automobile) survive as orphaned CDN files from the retired groupama-entreprises.fr. What IS published: one complete non-life contract set for local elected officials (Profil élus: dispositions générales 47 p. + IPID + tableau des montants et franchises + fiche L.112-2), the Groupama Gan Vie PRIIPs range (DIC/DIS/frais for Modulation, Premium, Capitalisation, Horizon Retraite, Nouvelle Vie PERIN, fonds euros, structured supports, Sérénité Obsèques) and the CCSF 'engagement lisibilité' benefit/reimbursement tables for santé and prévoyance — most of which print 'Document non contractuel' on their own face. Retail conditions générales are delivered only inside the quote tunnel (devis.groupama.fr, robots-blocked) and the authenticated espace client.

## Traps (do not re-derive)

1. Groupama does NOT publish conditions générales for its retail lines. Auto, habitation, santé individuelle, GAV, moto, vélo, scolaire, bateau, chien/chat, voyage, protection juridique, emprunteur: crawling all 1645 sitemap URLs produced zero CG, zero IPID and zero notice d'information for any of them. The CG are handed over inside the quote tunnel (devis.groupama.fr, robots 'Disallow: /') and in the espace client (espaceclient.groupama.fr, Keycloak login). Do not keep hunting for a retail library — there is none.
2. The agricole line, which is Groupama's historic core, publishes nothing contractual at all. /assurance-agricole/ has 86 sitemap URLs (multirisque agricole, récolte/chiffre d'affaires cultures, prairies, matériels et tracteurs, bâtiments, RC professionnelle, méthanisation…) and every PDF linked from them is a prevention brochure, a flyer or a third-party statistic. Nothing with the 'agricole' branch slug exists in this result.
3. robots.txt disallows /documents/* — that is the dead Liferay CMS path, not a document library. https://www.groupama.fr/documents/ returns 502 and every archived /documents/20282/… URL 502s too. The equally tempting legacy path /file/multimediaelement/… (which in 2009-2016 did host real CG: 'conditions générales conduire', 'conditions générales gav', 'conditions générales privatis', 'conditions generales gsa') is a clean 404 today. Both were checked; do not re-derive.
4. groupama-entreprises.fr 301s to groupama.fr/assurance-professionnels/, but three of its contractual PDFs are still served from the Contentful CDN and are the only full non-life Conditions Générales Groupama publishes anywhere: Assurance Flotte Automobile CG (87 p.), its Annexe Protection Juridique Automobile and its Convention Spéciale Assistance > 3,5 T. ## Two documents removed: they are securities, not insurance

`DIC-Telluma-Groupama-Boost-septembre-2026.pdf` and `DIC-Taux-E12m-Groupama-septembre-2026.pdf`
are **PRIIPs KIDs for structured notes** (*titres de créance*) issued by **Goldman Sachs Finance
Corp International Ltd**, guaranteed by The Goldman Sachs Group Inc., supervised by the **AMF**.
Not insurance contracts, not ACPR-supervised, not Groupama's risk.

The first contains the word "Groupama" **zero times**; in the second it appears only inside a
commercial product name. They sit in Groupama's Contentful asset library because such notes are
distributed as unit-linked supports inside its life contracts — which is exactly how a securities
document comes to look like an insurance one.

Removed from `sources/fr/groupama.yml` and from the extraction set. Recorded here rather than
silently dropped, and worth remembering as a shape: **an insurer's media library also holds the
paperwork of the instruments inside its contracts.** A DIC on an insurer's own CDN is not
necessarily an insurance document.
