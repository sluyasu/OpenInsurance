## Résumé

General Insurance Conditions of DKV Luxembourg S.A. for BUSINESS TRAVEL, a business-travel medical and assistance insurance sold to a legal person established in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg for its employees. The Policyholder buys a package of 250 insured travel days, to be claimed within three years. Cover applies worldwide outside Luxembourg for outpatient, inpatient and dental treatment and search and rescue costs up to a combined 150,000 EUR per Trip and per person, plus return transport and repatriation at full cost. The maximum duration is 99 days per Insured Person and per calendar year.

## Durée & résiliation

- Durée : The insured travel days purchased are to be claimed within a period of three years from the entry into force of the contract. The maximum duration is 99 days per Insured Person and per calendar year.
- Droit spécial : The insurance protection of the Insured Person begins upon the start of the insured and reported Trip by the Policyholder, but not before the actual payment of the premium or prior to the conclusion of the contract. The Trip begins at the time which the Insured Person leaves his/her ordinary residence and/or ordinary place of work. - PORTEUR DU RISQUE ETABLI ET NOMME. Cette mention est la definition contractuelle du terme INSURANCE COMPANY, c'est-a-dire de la partie qui garantit les prestations ('The Insurance Company guarantees the benefits defined in the present General Insurance Conditions'). - Comptage des puces ferme exactement.
