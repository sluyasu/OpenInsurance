## Résumé

Insurance product information document (IPID) for the easyPROTECT - Discover service distributed under the LALUX Assurances brand. The service is a package of guarantees for insured persons whose main residence is in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, aged between 15 and 27 years and preparing to begin their professional life (students, apprentices and similar categories). The basic package comprises civil liability for private and student life excluding professional activities — with a "Student accommodation" extension for tenants of student housing located in Europe — and legal protection. Mobile IT coverage, Assistance (tuition and apprenticeship fees, return costs, recovery of lost computer data) and Medical emergency (outpatient treatment, dental care, hospitalisation abroad and repatriation) are available as options for an additional premium, each subject to a stated monetary cap.

## Garanties

### Basic package — Civil liability - p. 1
Civil liability: e.g. damage caused during private and student life excluding professional activities, damage caused as a keeper or guardian of domestic animals or damage caused by water resulting from burst water pipes.
- Optionnelle : non · Portée : worldwide
  - Condition : Cover is for private and student life, excluding professional activities.

### Basic package — Civil liability, “Student accommodation” extension - p. 1
“Student accommodation” extension: coverage granted to the policyholder who, as a tenant, occupies student housing located in Europe, e.g. for material damage caused to the rented building as a result of fire, water damage or glass breakage or material damage to the rented furniture or furniture belonging to the lessee and located in the rented housing (e.g. theft).
- Optionnelle : non · Portée : student accommodation located in Europe · Limite : Material damage to furniture resulting from theft limited to EUR 3,000.
  - Sous-limite : Material damage to furniture resulting from theft: limited to EUR 3,000
  - Condition : Granted to the policyholder who, as a tenant, occupies student housing located in Europe.

### Basic package — Legal protection - p. 1
Legal protection: e.g. costs and fees for all procedures, investigations, expert opinions and proceedings following the occurrence of damage causing either death or material/bodily damage (criminal proceedings brought against the insured person or actions to be brought against any person liable for the damage suffered by the insured person).
- Optionnelle : non · Portée : worldwide (Legal protection relating to civil liability)
  - Condition : Free choice of lawyer in Luxembourg. For abroad, the prior agreement of the insurance company is required.

### Option — Mobile IT coverage - p. 1
Mobile IT coverage: breakage and theft of computer and non-portable audiovisual equipment inside the home; breakage and theft of computer/portable audio-visual/ portable telephone equipment worldwide.
- Optionnelle : oui · Portée : Inside the home for computer and non-portable audiovisual equipment; worldwide for computer, portable audio-visual and portable telephone equipment. · Limite : For breakage and theft, the intervention is limited to EUR 284 (index 100) per claim and per year.
  - Sous-limite : Breakage and theft: EUR 284 (index 100) per claim and per year
  - Condition : Available as an option and for an additional premium.

### Option — Assistance - p. 1
Assistance: reimbursement of tuition and apprenticeship fees (in the event of interruption of studies due to illness, accident or death of the insured person or their parents), reimbursement of return costs, and recovery of lost computer data (stored on a desktop or laptop computer).
- Optionnelle : oui · Portée : worldwide · Limite : Reimbursement of tuition and apprenticeship fees limited to EUR 2,000 per year and per claim; maximum return costs of EUR 500 per claim per year.
  - Sous-limite : Tuition and apprenticeship fees: limited to EUR 2,000 per year and per claim
  - Sous-limite : Return costs: maximum EUR 500 per claim per year
  - Condition : Available as an option and for an additional premium.
  - Condition : Reimbursement of tuition and apprenticeship fees applies in the event of interruption of studies due to illness, accident or death of the insured person or their parents.
  - Condition : Recovery of lost computer data applies to data stored on a desktop or laptop computer.

### Option — Medical emergency - p. 1
Medical emergency: In the event of accident, illness or death, the Company guarantees, within the limits provided for in the General Terms and Conditions, outpatient treatment, dental care and hospitalisation abroad, as well as repatriation (if medically necessary).
- Optionnelle : oui · Portée : European Union, with the exception of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg and the United Kingdom · Limite : Within the limits provided for in the General Terms and Conditions; for dental treatment, temporary prostheses are limited to EUR 250 per prosthesis.
  - Sous-limite : Dental treatment, temporary prostheses: limited to EUR 250 per prosthesis
  - Condition : Available as an option and for an additional premium.
  - Condition : Repatriation only if medically necessary.
